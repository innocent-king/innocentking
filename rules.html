⚽ Football Rules Guide

Saare Major Rules - Aasan Urdu Me • Referee Ke Liye Full Guide

1. Match Ka Basic Structure

➕
BASIC
  • Players: Har team me 11 players, 1 Goalkeeper lazmi hai.
  • Time: Match 90 minute ka hota hai, 45-45 minute ke 2 half.
  • Half Time Break: 15 minute ka break hota hai.
  • Extra Time: Agar barabar ho aur knockout match ho to 30 minute extra, 15-15 ke 2 half.
  • Substitution: Aam tor par 5 substitutions allowed hain, 3 moko me.
  • Jeet: Jo team zyada goal karegi wo jeetegi.
Misal: Agar 90 minute ke baad score 2-2 hai aur ye final hai, to pehle 30 minute Extra Time hoga, phir bhi barabar raha to Penalties hongi.

2. Kick Off / Match Shuru

➕
BASIC
  • Toss se decide hota hai kon si team pehle kick off karegi aur kon si side legi.
  • Kick off center circle se hota hai, ball aage ya peeche ja sakti hai.
  • Goal ke baad bhi dobara center se kick off hota hai, jis team ne goal khaya ho wo kick off karti hai.
  • Kick Off se seedha dusri team ko goal ho sakta hai, apni team ko nahi. Agar ball seedhi dusre ke goal me chali jaye to goal hai. Agar apne hi goal me chali jaye to goal nahi, Corner milega dusri team ko.

3. Offside - Sab Se Mushkil Rule, Aasan Me

➕
OFFSIDE

Seedha Matlab: Agar tumhare sathi ne pass diya aur us waqt tum sab se aage ho, tumhare aur goal ke beech me sirf Goalkeeper bacha hai (ya us se bhi kam), to tum Offside ho.

  • Offside ke liye 2 players ka hona zaroori hai, aam tor pe Keeper + 1 Defender. Pass lagte waqt tumhare aur goal ke darmiyan 2 opposite team ke bande hone chahiye.
  • Offside kab hota hai? Jab pass dene ke waqt tum opponent ke 2nd last player se aage ho.
  • Apne Half me kabhi Offside nahi hota.
  • Throw-In, Corner, Goal Kick par Offside nahi hota.
  • Sirf khara hona Offside nahi hai, jab tak tum ball ko touch na karo ya khel me dakhal na do.
  • Offside par Indirect Free Kick milti hai, jahan se Offside hua tha.
Misal: Ali ne Ahmed ko pass diya. Pass ke waqt Ahmed defender se 2 kadam aage tha aur sirf Keeper uske samne tha. Ahmed ne ball pakri = Offside. Agar Ahmed ball ko chor deta aur peeche wala player aake leta, to Offside nahi.
✅ Apne half me ho = No Offside
❌ Pass ke waqt sab se aage = Offside

4. Foul Kya Hota Hai?

➕
FOUL
  • Kisi player ko dhakka dena, tang marna, pakarna, hath se rokna.
  • Ball ke bajaye player ko kick marna.
  • Khatarnak tareeqe se tackle karna.
  • Opponent par thookna, gaali dena, badtameezi karna.

Saza: Foul jahan hua, wahan se Direct Free Kick milti hai. Agar foul apne Penalty Box me hua to Penalty milti hai.

5. Handball Rule

➕
HANDBALL
  • Agar ball hath ya bazu ko lag jaye aur hath jism se bahar phaila hua ho to Handball hai.
  • Agar ball hath se goal ki taraf ja rahi thi aur hath ne roka, to Handball hai.
  • Agar hath jism ke sath chipka hua hai aur ball aake lagi, to aksar Handball nahi diya jata.
  • Goalkeeper apne box ke bahar hath nahi laga sakta.
  • Handball se agar goal hua to goal cancel ho jayega.
Misal: Defender ne hath phaila kar ball roki jo goal me ja rahi thi = Handball + Penalty + Yellow/Red Card.

6. Yellow Card / Red Card

➕
YELLOW RED

Yellow Card Kab Milta Hai?

  • Bar bar foul karna.
  • Time zaya karna.
  • Referee se behas karna.
  • Shirt utar kar goal celebrate karna.
  • Jaankari ke liye jhooti diving karna.

Red Card Kab Milta Hai?

  • 2 Yellow Card = 1 Red Card, player bahar.
  • Seedha Red: Last player ko goal se rokna, khatarnak tackle, mukka marna, gaali dena.
  • Red Card wala player wapas nahi aa sakta, team 10 players se khelti hai.

7. Free Kick - Direct Aur Indirect

➕
FREE KICK

Direct Free Kick: Is se seedha goal ho sakta hai. Foul, Handball par milti hai.

Indirect Free Kick: Is se seedha goal nahi ho sakta, pehle kisi aur player ko touch karna lazmi hai. Offside, Goalkeeper ka ball zyada der pakarna, khatarnak khel par milti hai.

Referee Indirect ke liye hath upar uthata hai.

⭐ 8. Penalty Rules - Alag Se Poora Section

➕
PENALTY

Penalty Kab Milti Hai?

  • Jab koi player apne Penalty Box (Dabba) ke andar Foul kare.
  • Jab apne Box ke andar Handball ho.
  • Goalkeeper apne Box me kisi ko giraye to bhi Penalty.

Penalty Kick Ka Tareeqa:

  • Ball Penalty Spot par rakhi jati hai, jo goal se 11 meter (12 yard) door hota hai.
  • Sirf Kick marne wala aur Goalkeeper hota hai, baaki sab players Box ke bahar aur D ke peeche hote hain.
  • Referee ki seeti par hi kick marni hai, pehle nahi.
  • Kick marne wala ball ko aage ki taraf hi marega, peeche nahi.
  • Goalkeeper line par hona chahiye, kick se pehle aage nahi aa sakta. Ek paon line par hona lazmi hai.

Agar Goalkeeper Aage Aa Gaya? Kick dobara hogi, aur Keeper ko Yellow Card mil sakta hai.

Agar Koi Player Pehle Andar Aa Gaya?

  • Agar goal ho gaya aur attacker pehle andar aya tha to kick dobara hogi.
  • Agar miss hui aur defender pehle andar aya tha to kick dobara hogi.

⭐ Penalty Shootout Kab Hota Hai?

  • Jab 90 minute + Extra Time ke baad bhi match barabar ho aur knockout / final ho.
  • Shootout match ka hissa nahi hota, ye sirf winner nikalne ke liye hota hai. Shootout ke goal match ke score me add nahi hote, sirf shootout ka result likha jata hai. Misal: Match 2-2, Penalties 4-3.

⭐ Shootout Shuru Hone Se Pehle - Toss

  • Referee toss karta hai ke kis goal par penalties hongi.
  • Dobara toss hota hai ke pehli kick kon si team maregi. Jeetne wali team decide karti hai pehle marna hai ya baad me.

⭐ Kon Kon Penalty Laga Sakta Hai?

  • Sirf wo players jo match / Extra Time khatam hone par ground me the. Bench par betha player, ya Red Card wala player, ya pehle se bahar gaya player penalty nahi maar sakta.
  • Goalkeeper bhi Penalty laga sakta hai! HAAN. Goalkeeper bhi team ka player hai. Wo bhi apni bari par penalty maar sakta hai, aur aksar 5th ya Sudden Death me Keeper hi marta hai.
  • Goalkeeper shootout me bhi Goalkeeper hi rahega, lekin jab uski bari ayegi to wo penalty marne jayega, aur wapas aake phir Keeper ban jayega.
  • Agar kisi team ka player kam hai (misal 10 vs 11), to dosri team ko bhi apne players kam karne honge taake dono ke players barabar ho jayen. Isko "Reduce to Equate" kehte hain.

⭐ Penalty Marne Ka Order

  • Dono teams 5-5 penalties marti hain, bari bari. Pehle Team A, phir Team B, phir A, phir B... isi tarah.
  • Agar beech me hi jeet pakki ho jaye to shootout wahin khatam ho jayega, 5-5 poore karne zaroori nahi hain. Misal: Team A 3-0 aage hai aur Team B ke sirf 2 kicks bache hain, to Team A jeet jayegi.
  • Har player sirf 1 dafa penalty marega pehle 5 me. Ek player dobara nahi maar sakta jab tak uski team ke saare players na maar chuke hon.
  • 5-5 ke baad barabar ho to Sudden Death, 1-1 kar ke chalta rahega.
  • Sudden Death me bhi wahi rule hai, pehle saare bache hue players, phir dobara shuru se.
  • Order pehle se likh kar dena zaroori nahi hai, Captain / Coach har bari bata sakta hai kon marega.

⭐ Shootout Me Goalkeeper Ke Rules

  • Goalkeeper ko shootout me change kiya ja sakta hai, lekin sirf tab jab wo zakhmi ho jaye, ya pehle se ground me maujood kisi player se. Bench se naya Keeper nahi aa sakta agar substitutions khatam ho gayi hon.
  • Goalkeeper ko penalty ke waqt Goal Line par hona hai, ek paon line par lazmi hai. Wo idhar udhar move kar sakta hai, lekin aage nahi aa sakta kick se pehle.
  • Goalkeeper kick marne wale ko tang nahi kar sakta, na post ko hila sakta hai, na bekaar me late kar sakta hai. Aisa kiya to Yellow Card.
  • Agar Goalkeeper ko Shootout me Red Card mil jaye to uski jagah ground me maujood koi aur player Keeper banega, aur wo team ek player kam se shootout khelti rahegi.

⭐ Shootout Me Kya Allowed Nahi Hai?

  • Kick marne wala ruk ruk kar (feint) kar sakta hai, lekin kick ke aakhri step par rukna / dhoka dena mana hai. Aisa kiya to Yellow Card + kick dobara nahi milegi, miss gini jayegi.
  • Penalty ke baad rebound nahi hota shootout me. Ball ek dafa kick hui, agar Keeper ne rok li ya post lagi to kick khatam. Dobara ball ko touch nahi kar sakte.
  • Baaki saare players Center Circle me khare honge, Box ke paas nahi.

⭐ Haar Jeet Ka Faisla Kon Karega?

  • Haar Jeet ka aakhri faisla Referee karega. App khud winner decide nahi karegi.
  • Referee score dekh kar batayega kon jeeta.
Misal: Defender ne Box me shirt pakar kar giraya = Penalty + Yellow Card. Keeper ne line se pehle jump mara aur ball rok li = Penalty Dobara.

Shootout Misal: Team A ke 5 players ne maar li, Keeper abhi bacha hai. Sudden Death me Team A ka Keeper bhi penalty maar sakta hai = Bilkul Jaiz Hai.

9. Corner Kick

➕
CORNER
  • Jab defending team ka player ball ko apni Goal Line se bahar nikal de (Goal ke baghair) to Corner milta hai.
  • Corner, corner wale jhande ke paas se liya jata hai.
  • Corner se seedha goal ho sakta hai, isko Olympic Goal kehte hain.
  • Corner par Offside nahi hota.

10. Goal Kick

➕
GOAL KICK
  • Jab attacking team ka player ball ko Goal Line se bahar nikal de to Goal Kick milti hai.
  • Goalkeeper ya defender Goal Area se ball ko kick karta hai.
  • Goal Kick par bhi Offside nahi hota.
  • Goal Kick se seedha goal ho sakta hai.

11. Throw-In

➕
THROW-IN
  • Jab ball side line se bahar jaye to Throw-In milta hai.
  • Dono hath se, sar ke peeche se ball phenkni hai.
  • Dono paon zameen par hone chahiye.
  • Throw-In se seedha goal nahi ho sakta.
  • Throw-In par Offside nahi hota.
  • Galat Throw-In hua to dosri team ko mil jayega.

12. Goalkeeper Ke Khas Rules

➕
KEEPER
  • Keeper sirf apne Penalty Box ke andar hath laga sakta hai.
  • Keeper ball ko hath me 8 second se zyada nahi rakh sakta.
  • Apne hi player ne paon se Back Pass diya to Keeper hath se nahi pakar sakta, paon se khelna hoga.
  • Throw-In se aayi hui ball Keeper hath se nahi pakar sakta.
  • Agar Keeper Box ke bahar hath lagaye to Handball + Free Kick / Red Card bhi ho sakta hai.
  • Keeper ka kapron ka rang baaki players se alag hona chahiye.

14. VAR Kya Hai?

➕
VAR

VAR matlab Video Assistant Referee. Bara match me camera se check karte hain.

  • Goal hua ya nahi?
  • Penalty thi ya nahi?
  • Red Card banta tha ya nahi?
  • Offside tha ya nahi?

Referee screen par ja kar khud bhi dekh sakta hai. Aakhri faisla ground wale Referee ka hi hota hai.

15. Diving / Natak Karna

➕
DIVING

Agar player baghair foul ke khud gir jaye taake Penalty ya Free Kick mil jaye, isko Diving kehte hain.

Saza: Yellow Card + dosri team ko Free Kick.

16. Advantage Rule

➕
ADVANTAGE

Agar foul hua lekin jis team par foul hua usko faida ho raha hai (wo attack par hai), to Referee khel nahi rokta, khel chalne deta hai. Isko Advantage kehte hain.

Misal: Defender ne foul kiya, lekin ball attacker ke paas hi reh gayi aur wo goal ki taraf ja raha hai, to Referee seeti nahi bajayega.

17. Ball Andar Hai Ya Bahar? Goal Kab Hota Hai?

➕
GOAL / BALL OUT
  • Ball Bahar Kab Hoti Hai? Jab poori ki poori ball line se bahar nikal jaye. Thori si bhi line par hai to andar hi gini jayegi.
  • Goal Kab Hota Hai? Jab poori ball goal line ko cross kar ke goal ke andar chali jaye, aur us se pehle koi foul / offside / handball na hua ho.
  • Ball agar post se lag kar wapas aaye to khel jari rahega, goal nahi hoga.
  • Referee ki seeti ke baad hi khel rukta hai, ball bahar jane par Linesman jhanda uthata hai.
Misal: Ball ka 90% hissa line se bahar hai lekin 10% line par hai = Ball Andar Hai, Khel Jari.

18. Free Kick Me Deewar (Wall) Kitni Door?

➕
WALL
  • Free Kick par defending team ki deewar ball se 9.15 meter (10 yard) door honi chahiye.
  • Referee spray se nishan lagata hai ya kadam se naap kar batata hai.
  • Deewar ke players hath se apna chehra / jism bacha sakte hain, lekin hath phaila kar ball nahi rok sakte, warna Handball + Penalty / Free Kick dobara.
  • Attacking team ka player deewar ke 1 meter ke andar khara nahi ho sakta, warna Free Kick dosri team ko mil jayegi.
  • Quick Free Kick bhi li ja sakti hai agar Referee ne seeti ka wait karne ko na kaha ho.

19. Drop Ball - Khel Dobara Kaise Shuru Hota Hai?

➕
DROP BALL
  • Agar Referee ne khel kisi wajah se roka ho jese player zakhmi ho gaya, ball phat gayi, bahar se koi cheez andar aa gayi, to khel Drop Ball se shuru hota hai.
  • Ball usi jagah drop ki jati hai jahan khel ruka tha.
  • Ball usi team ko di jati hai jiske paas ball thi jab khel ruka tha.
  • Baaki players Drop Ball se 4 meter door honge.
  • Drop Ball se seedha goal nahi ho sakta, pehle kisi player ko touch karna lazmi hai.

20. Added Time / Injury Time Kya Hota Hai?

➕
ADDED TIME
  • Match 90 minute ka hai, lekin agar beech me time zaya hua ho to Referee uska time aakhir me add karta hai. Isko Added Time kehte hain.
  • Time kis cheez par zaya hota hai? Substitution, zakhmi player ka ilaj, time waste karna, VAR check, goal celebrate me zyada time lagana.
  • 4th Official board par batata hai kitne minute add hue hain. Misal: +5 minute.
  • Added Time ke baad bhi Referee chahe to thora aur time de sakta hai agar usi dauran phir time zaya ho.
  • Half Time ke baad bhi Added Time ho sakta hai.

21. Khatarnak Khel - High Kick / Bicycle Kick

➕
DANGEROUS PLAY
  • Agar player paon itna upar uthaye ke samne wale player ko lagne ka khatra ho, to ye Khatarnak Khel hai, chahe paon laga ya na laga.
  • Bicycle Kick / Overhead Kick jaiz hai, lekin sirf tab jab paas me koi opponent na ho. Agar opponent paas hai to foul hai.
  • Is par Indirect Free Kick milti hai, agar paon lag gaya to Direct Free Kick + Card bhi mil sakta hai.
  • Sar neeche kar ke ball ke liye jana bhi khatarnak hai.
Misal: Defender header marne aaya aur attacker ne paon upar kar diya, paon nahi laga lekin khatra tha = Indirect Free Kick.

22. Obstruction / Rasta Rokna

➕
OBSTRUCTION
  • Agar player baghair ball ke, sirf apne jism se opponent ka rasta roke taake wo ball tak na ja sake, to ye Obstruction hai.
  • Ball ko jism se shield karna jaiz hai, lekin sirf tab jab ball khelne ke fasle me ho (1-2 kadam).
  • Corner ke waqt Goalkeeper ke samne khare ho kar usko hilne se rokna bhi foul hai.
  • Saza: Indirect Free Kick.

23. Goalkeeper Ka 8 Second Wala Naya Rule

➕
KEEPER - NAYA RULE
  • Pehle Keeper 6 second tak ball rakh sakta tha, ab naye rule me 8 second tak rakh sakta hai.
  • Agar Keeper 8 second se zyada ball hath me rakhe to dosri team ko Corner Kick milegi, Free Kick nahi.
  • Referee aakhri 5 second ungli se gin kar batayega.
  • Ye rule time waste rokne ke liye banaya gaya hai.
  • Back Pass wala rule abhi bhi same hai: Apne player ne paon se pass diya to Keeper hath se nahi pakar sakta.

24. Player Ka Kit / Kapre Aur Saman

➕
KIT
  • Lazmi Saman: Shirt, Short, Socks, Shin Guard (Shin Pad), Shoes. Shin Guard ke baghair khelna mana hai.
  • Dono teams ke kapron ka rang alag alag hona chahiye taake pehchan me aaye.
  • Goalkeeper ka rang dono teams se alag hona chahiye.
  • Player koi khatarnak cheez nahi pehen sakta, jese kaan ki baali, anghooti, ghari, bracelet.
  • Shirt ke neeche siyasi ya ghalat likhai wala kapra pehenna mana hai.
  • Shirt utar kar goal celebrate karna Yellow Card hai.

25. Referee, Linesman Aur 4th Official Ka Kaam

➕
OFFICIALS

Main Referee: Ground ke andar sab se bara faisla uska hai. Seeti, Card, Penalty, Goal sab wo hi deta hai.

Linesman (Assistant Referee) - 2 hote hain:

  • Ball bahar gayi ya andar hai, ye batana.
  • Offside ka jhanda uthana.
  • Corner hai ya Goal Kick, ye batana.
  • Foul jo Referee ne nahi dekha, uska ishara dena.
  • Linesman ka faisla aakhri nahi hota, Referee chahe to usko na mane.

4th Official: Ground ke bahar hota hai. Substitution ka board dikhata hai, Added Time batata hai, Coach ko samjhata hai.

26. Referee Ke Ishare - Haath Se Kya Matlab Hai?

➕
SIGNALS
  • Hath aage ki taraf: Direct Free Kick hai.
  • Hath upar ki taraf: Indirect Free Kick hai, jab tak ball kisi aur ko na lage hath upar rahega.
  • Penalty Spot ki taraf ungli: Penalty hai.
  • Corner Flag ki taraf ungli: Corner hai.
  • Goal Kick ki taraf ungli: Goal Kick hai.
  • Dono hath se faida ka ishara (aage bhago): Advantage hai, khel jari rakho.
  • Seeti + Red Card upar: Player bahar.
  • Seeti + Haath se ghari ki taraf ishara: Time waste mat karo.

27. Zakhmi Player Ka Rule

➕
INJURY
  • Agar player halka zakhmi hai to khel chalta rahega, Referee khel nahi rokega.
  • Agar zakham serious hai, sar par chot hai, to Referee foran khel rok dega.
  • Zakhmi player ka ilaj ground ke bahar hoga, ground ke andar nahi, siwaye Goalkeeper ke.
  • Ilaaj ke baad player ko Referee ki ijazat se wapas andar aana hoga.
  • Agar koi player jaan boojh kar late raha ke time zaya ho, to usko Yellow Card milega.
  • Ek team ka player zakhmi hai to dosri team ko ball bahar phenkna zaroori nahi hai, ye insaniat hai, rule nahi hai.

28. DOGSO - Aakhri Player Ko Rokna

➕
RED CARD RULE

DOGSO matlab: Denying Obvious Goal Scoring Opportunity. Matlab saaf goal rokna.

  • Agar defender ne aakhri player ko foul kar ke saaf goal roka, to Red Card milega.
  • Lekin agar defender ne ball ke liye koshish ki thi aur galti se foul ho gaya Box ke andar, to sirf Yellow Card + Penalty milega, Red nahi.
  • Agar defender ne hath se goal roka, ya shirt pakar kar giraya, ya baghair ball ke giraya, to seedha Red Card.
Misal: Attacker akele Keeper ki taraf ja raha tha, defender ne peeche se pakar kar gira diya = Red Card + Free Kick.

29. Captain Ka Kaam Kya Hai?

➕
CAPTAIN
  • Captain sirf team ka leader hota hai, usko koi khas power nahi hoti ke wo Referee ka faisla badal de.
  • Captain hi Toss karta hai.
  • Captain hi Referee se izzat se baat kar sakta hai, baaki players behas karenge to Card milega.
  • Naye rule me sirf Captain hi Referee se baat kar sakta hai, baaki player aaye to Yellow Card.
  • Captain ka band (armband) pehenna lazmi hai.

30. Match Kab Rukta Hai? Kab Khatam Hota Hai?

➕
MATCH END
  • Match sirf Referee ki aakhri seeti par khatam hota hai, ghari dekh kar nahi.
  • Agar Free Kick ya Penalty reh gayi ho to Referee usko poora karwa kar hi seeti bajayega.
  • Agar kisi team ke 7 se kam players reh jayen (misal 6 players), to match khatam kar diya jayega, dosri team jeet jayegi.
  • Toofan, andhera, ya ground kharab hone par Referee match rok sakta hai ya dobara karwa sakta hai.
  • Match khatam hone ke baad bhi Referee Red Card de sakta hai agar player ne badtameezi ki.
